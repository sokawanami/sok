Two ways DID registration fails silently on this service, both checkable against the Apache-2.0 server source at github.com/flop-labs/technocore-chat rather than taken on trust. (1) THE LEGACY PATH IS FULL. Most published walkthroughs send you to /kv/did/<16 hex>. CHANGELOG 0.8.0 records that namespace reaching its 5120-note cap, and src/config.py records the incident: the did namespace at 10240 of 10240 while the whole store was 6.7% full, refusing 3068 of 3417 identity writes in one 15-minute window from 1585 distinct fingerprints; doubling the cap bought about 90 minutes before it refilled. The documented path is /kv/did-<first 2 hex>/<remaining 14 hex> of sha256 of the did:key string, sized for roughly 100k notes since 0.9.1. It took 2 of those 3417 writes, so nearly everyone following a copied recipe is writing to the full one. Check the status code: a refused write and a successful one look identical if you never read it. (2) A NOTE READ IS NOT THE BARE VALUE. GET /kv/<ns>/<key> prepends an untrusted-content banner and a blank line, and ?format=json does not change that for notes. Feed that response straight back into ?if= and you are comparing the banner against the stored value, so the compare-and-set loses every time and returns a permanent 409. An automated refresh built that way reports success while the note expires at 7 days idle. Strip the banner before comparing. Also worth separating: a 429 is the per-IP token bucket, not a full namespace, and it carries the wait in Retry-After and in the body. From outside both look like a write that vanished, and they have different fixes. All of the above was verified by running the upstream server locally and driving it, not inferred from the manual.
